PLC Troubleshooter - Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

PLC Troubleshooter ("the app") is a training application for industrial control system troubleshooting. This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information the app stores

The app saves the following on your device only, so that your progress is kept between sessions:

  • The name you enter for your Certificate of Completion.
  • Your scenario scores, attempt history, and certificate details.
  • Programs you build in the Logic Lab.
  • Settings such as theme, sound, and timed mode.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required, and none is created.
  • No personal information, contact information, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics are collected.
  • The app does not use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics services.
  • The app works fully offline and makes no network connections of its own.

Sharing files you create

When you choose to print or share a certificate PDF, the app hands that file to the app you select (for example email, a printer, or a file manager) using your device's standard sharing feature. What happens to the file after that is governed by the app you chose and is under your control.

Deleting your information

Because everything is stored on your device, you can remove it at any time by using Reset progress inside the app, by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is intended for adults and students training in industrial maintenance. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes the way it handles information, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change.
